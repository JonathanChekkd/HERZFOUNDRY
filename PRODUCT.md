# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Prospective clients evaluating Bayu Hermawan for product animation, CGI advertising, and 3D visualization work.
- Creative and brand teams who need to understand the quality of the work, the production process, and how to start a project.

## Product Purpose

HERZ FOUNDRY is Bayu Hermawan's portfolio and project-inquiry surface. It should make the work immediately tangible, establish Bayu as the creator behind it, explain how projects move from direction to delivery, and make direct contact effortless.

Success means a first-time visitor can quickly understand what HERZ FOUNDRY does, see credible work and process evidence, and confidently start a conversation by email or WhatsApp.

## Positioning

HERZ FOUNDRY presents product visualization as intentional creative production rather than decoration: every frame is developed to help a physical product reach the right audience with precision.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive on a public, scroll-based portfolio and may evaluate it on desktop or mobile.
- The primary journey is work sample to production process to creator profile to direct inquiry.
- The site is published through GitHub Pages from a static repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current information architecture and factual content: work, workflow, Bayu's profile, featured clients, and contact channels.
- Preserve working email, WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, and Threads destinations.
- The implementation remains a fast static site with no required backend or build step.
- The experience must work with keyboard navigation, touch, reduced motion, and common mobile and desktop widths.
- Do not invent new clients, testimonials, awards, performance claims, or project outcomes.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the HERZ FOUNDRY name, Bayu Hermawan attribution, dark theme, current portfolio media, client marks, and direct, precise voice.
- The visual identity may be rebuilt, with restrained gradients and a cinematic product-imaging character, while remaining recognizably HERZ FOUNDRY.

## Evidence on Hand

- `index.html`: current product copy, information architecture, links, interactions, and deployment surface.
- `assets/image1.png` through `assets/image4.png`: static product visualizations.
- `assets/video1.mp4` through `assets/video4.mp4` and their thumbnails: motion work.
- `assets/before1.png`, `assets/after1.png`, `assets/before2.png`, and `assets/after2.png`: production breakdown evidence.
- `assets/profile-pic-bayu.png`: creator portrait.
- `assets/client1.png` through `assets/client10.png`: featured-client marks.
- No testimonials, case-study outcomes, awards, or quantified business results are supplied; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the work itself and make it visible without waiting on ornamental effects.
2. Turn production rigor into evidence through real process and before/after material.
3. Keep the path from interest to direct conversation obvious and low-friction.
4. Treat performance, accessibility, and responsive behavior as part of the portfolio's craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction behavior, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and headings, useful alternative text, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable from narrow mobile screens through large desktop displays.
